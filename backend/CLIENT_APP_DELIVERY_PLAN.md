# Miracle Me App Delivery Plan

We will rebuild the Miracle Me mobile app with a refreshed design for customers and practitioners. Both will use the same app, with features suited to their role.

The app will include chat, audio calls, and video calls. We will build video calling after the chat and audio experiences have been tested. The backend and admin panel will be developed separately alongside the app.

The following is a proposed sequence of work. We will confirm the dates after reviewing the detailed requirements and coordinating with the backend team. At each stage, we will share a build or demonstration for your feedback.

## Week 1: Sign-in and finding a practitioner

**For customers**

- A refreshed app design and easy-to-use navigation.
- Sign in using a mobile number and verification code.
- Browse and search practitioners, view their profiles, and save favorites.

**For practitioners**

- Sign in and access the initial practitioner screens.

**What we will show you:** The new design and the customer journey from sign-in to finding a practitioner.

## Week 2: Chat

**For customers**

- Request a chat and see pricing and available free time.
- Exchange messages and return to previous conversations.

**For practitioners**

- Receive and respond to chat requests.
- Exchange messages and view conversations.

**What we will show you:** A customer and a practitioner requesting a chat and exchanging messages.

## Week 3: Audio calls

**For customers**

- Request an audio call and see its connection status.
- Mute or end the call and see its duration.

**For practitioners**

- Set availability and accept or decline call requests.
- Join, mute, and end audio calls.

**What we will show you:** An audio call between a customer and a practitioner, including declined and interrupted calls.

## Week 4: Payments and earnings

**For customers**

- View wallet balance and recharge the wallet.
- See payment and usage history.

**For practitioners**

- View earnings and session history.
- Add payout details and request a withdrawal, based on the agreed payout process.

**What we will show you:** The customer recharge journey and the practitioner earnings screens.

## Week 5: Account and notifications

**For customers and practitioners**

- Profile, settings, help, and logout.
- Notifications for messages and requests.
- Account-deletion request and blocking features.
- Improvements to screen design and messages shown when something goes wrong.

**What we will show you:** The account and notification journeys in a more complete app build.

## Week 6: Video calls

**For customers and practitioners**

- Request, accept, and join a video call.
- Turn the camera and microphone on or off, switch cameras, and end the call.
- See call and connection status.

**What we will show you:** A video call between a customer and a practitioner, including the main call controls.

## Week 7: Video-call testing and refinement

- Test video calls on mobile devices, including weak connections, interruptions, and camera and microphone permissions.
- Fix issues and review the calling experience for both roles.

**What we will show you:** A refined video-calling experience ready for full app testing.

## Week 8: Testing and release preparation

- Test the complete customer and practitioner journeys on mobile devices.
- Check chat, audio and video calls, payments, notifications, and account features.
- Fix issues found during testing and share a release candidate for your review.

**What we will deliver:** A build of the complete app for client review before store submission.

## Schedule

This eight-week sequence is a **proposed plan**, not a confirmed deadline. We will agree on delivery dates after confirming the requirements, designs, and backend schedule. We will review progress regularly and communicate any changes to the plan.

The app work depends on backend features and payment and calling services being ready for integration and testing. App store review and approval happen after submission and are outside this schedule.

## Estimated audio and video calling costs

The app will need a calling service to connect customers and practitioners. Its monthly cost will depend mainly on how many calls are made, how long they last, and whether they use audio or video.

For illustration, the estimates below assume that the stated number of people are on calls at the same time for an average of **two hours a day, every day**. Half the calling time is audio and half is video. Each call has one customer and one practitioner, so 10 people means 5 calls at once.

| People on calls at the same time | Agora per month | 100ms per month |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 10 | About ₹4,400 | About ₹4,300 |
| 50 | About ₹19,900 | About ₹21,500 |
| 100 | About ₹37,200 | About ₹42,900 |

These are planning estimates, not quotes. The 100ms figures do not deduct its advertised free allowance, so its actual charges may be lower. Taxes, exchange-rate changes, hosting, payment processing, recording, and paid support are not included. More or fewer calls will change the cost.

We will compare Agora with 100ms, a service with an India-connected founding team, and confirm pricing and call quality before selecting a provider. The final choice will depend on the expected call volume, provider quotes, and testing.

Prices were reviewed on 1 November 2026 using an approximate exchange rate of ₹96 per US dollar. Sources: [Agora pricing](https://docs.agora.io/en/realtime-media/rtc/reference/pricing), [100ms pricing](https://www.100ms.live/pricing), and [NSE exchange-rate reference](https://www.nseindia.com/report-detail/rbi-reference-rate-statistics).
